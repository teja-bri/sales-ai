High probability opportunities above 70 percent should be prioritized for closing actions. Sales teams should confirm final proposal, decision-maker approval, and expected close date.

Medium probability opportunities between 40 and 69 percent need active follow-up. Sales teams should identify blockers, confirm budget, and schedule decision-maker meetings.

Low probability opportunities below 40 percent are risky. Sales teams should review customer urgency, budget fit, competitor involvement, and next-step clarity.

Digital signage opportunities often require technical clarification, site survey confirmation, content management discussion, and installation timeline planning.

Static signage opportunities often require artwork approval, material confirmation, production schedule planning, and delivery coordination.

A healthy sales pipeline should include clear opportunity stage, probability, estimated value, expected close date, and next action owner.
